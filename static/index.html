    Methodology & data sources
    • Historical: RMA County Yields × NOAA daily weather (avg precip mm, heat-stress days, GDD), merged at county-year-commodity level.
    • Model: LightGBM regressor predicting per-county yield from weather + categorical (state, county, commodity, irrigation). R² = 0.6440.
    • Anomaly definition: anomaly_flag = 1 when residual z-score ≥ 2 OR a NOAA weather anomaly is present.
    • Outcome categories: Normal · Extreme Weather Model Handled · Extreme Weather Yield Impact · Yield Mismatch / Unexplained.
    • 2024 predictions: Pure forecasts driven by NOAA window weather; no observed yields available, so they cannot be confirmed anomalies.
